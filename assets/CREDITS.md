# Visual assets

## Animated profile banner

`edward-mitsuru-clock.gif` combines the original geometric banner with a masked
section of the user-supplied video `mitsuru-kirijo-persona-3-reload-moewalls-com.mp4`.
The animation is confined to the burgundy area using a diagonal mask and a soft
fade behind the name. The text, divider, lower panel and moon remain separate
visual layers within the exported GIF; the README embeds a single image.

- Character: Mitsuru Kirijo, Persona 3 Reload (ATLUS / SEGA).
- Source filename references MoeWalls; animation creator has not been independently verified.
- Export: full 20-second loop, 1280 × 400, 12 fps, 112-color palette, no audio.
- Clock rotation is rendered into every frame: one revolution per 20-second loop.
- Clock artwork is scaled to 78% to keep its rotating ticks clear of the diagonal divider.
- The small star pulses every 4 seconds; the moon stays fixed.
- Original footage is kept locally and excluded from Git.
- This profile does not claim authorship of the source character art or animation.
